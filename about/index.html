About GovTech Nepal

An independent, open-source community building technology for Nepal’s public sector.

What we do

GovTech Nepal is the umbrella for open-source projects that make everyday public work simpler, more affordable and more Nepali: NepalOS, the Lipi keyboard, the Rasuwa Flood Bulletin and the Mountain fonts.

Independent

We are not a Government of Nepal office. We do not speak for any ministry and do not use the national emblem. Whether government adopts any of this work is government’s decision.

Who is behind it

GovTech Nepal was started by Niraj Bhusal and is open to everyone who wants to help.

Principles

  1. Open by default

    Code, designs and decisions are public, so anyone can check, reuse and improve them.

  2. Nepali first

    Nepali language, Unicode text and Bikram Sambat dates work properly from day one.

  3. Privacy and sovereignty

    No hidden tracking. Public data stays in Nepal, under Nepali control.

  4. Proven foundations

    We build on mature open-source projects such as Debian and KDE instead of reinventing them.

  5. Accessible to all

    Usable by people of all abilities, in Nepali and English, on modest hardware.

  6. Community-led

    Anyone can contribute. Plans and discussions happen in the open.

The name

“GovTech” is short for government technology. In Nepali we write it गभटेक नेपाल.

Open source, community-led. GovTech Nepal is an independent community project. It is not an office of the Government of Nepal and does not speak for it.
