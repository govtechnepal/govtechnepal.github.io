Typography

Mukta is used for everything: headings, body text and the wordmark. It covers Devanagari and Latin and is open source (SIL OFL). Kalimati is the font for official Nepali documents, used inside NepalOS, not on this site.

Usage

  • Keep clear space around the logo equal to half the height of the mark.
  • Don’t recolour, stretch, rotate or add effects.
  • Never combine the logo with the national emblem or flag.
  • Don’t use it in a way that suggests official government endorsement.
  • Minimum size: 16 px for the mark alone, 120 px wide for the full logo.
  • Crimson is an accent: keep it small.
