NepalOS

A free, modern, Nepali-first desktop for public offices and everyone else. Built on Debian 13 and KDE Plasma.

Try the web demoView the slides

What NepalOS is

NepalOS is a carefully configured Debian 13 (trixie) with the KDE Plasma desktop. It is not a new system written from scratch: it uses one of the most stable and widely trusted Linux distributions, with everything a Nepali office needs set up by default.

What’s inside

  • Modern KDE Plasma desktopClean light and dark looks, Himalaya and heritage wallpapers.
  • Google ChromeThe default browser, with managed bookmarks for public services.
  • ONLYOFFICE, Kalimati 12 ptThe default office suite; new documents start in Kalimati 12 pt.
  • Romanized and Traditional keyboardsBoth Nepali layouts, with a one-click switch.
  • GIOMS by defaultThe browser opens gioms.gov.np, the government’s integrated office management system.
  • Bikram Sambat dateThe clock shows the BS date in Nepali, alongside the AD date.

About Kalimati

Kalimati is the font used for official Nepali documents, so NepalOS uses it as the default document font. Kalimati is not under an open licence. Its redistribution rights must be confirmed before a public release, and the open Noto Sans Devanagari is the fallback.
